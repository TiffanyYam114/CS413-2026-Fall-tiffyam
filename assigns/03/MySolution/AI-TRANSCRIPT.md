AI used: ChatGPT

### Prompt 1: 
I didn't have any stakeholder answers for any of my questions, so I pasted my questions into ChatGPT and asked it to give me reasonable assumptions. Then I put these assumptions into my requirements specification.

### Prompt 2:

After completing parts 1 and 2, I pasted them into ChatGPT and asked it to create me a list of 20 functional requirements and 5 quality requirements.

ChatGPT made me a list, which I reviewed and added to my list if I also thought the requirement was necessary. For FR3, ChatGPT first said, "The system shall allow a user to create an editable copy of an example without modifying the original example." I changed "example" to "program" because users should be able to create copies of all LAMBDA programs, not just the examples.


### Prompt 3:

I asked ChatGPT to create acceptance criteria for FR3-FR7, FR9-FR13, FR15-FR19, since I thought those were the ones that could be most easily tested. Then I added the six that I thought were most important and covered a variety of requirements. 
Then I asked ChatGPT to create two failure scenarios and added them to the list.
